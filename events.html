Explore Any Event, Hands-Free

Walking a festival, street market, or event district? Put on your headphones and let CityYoYo narrate what's around you as you go — no schedule, no map, no searching.

How it works

  • Open Tour Mode and press your headphone button to start
  • CityYoYo uses your location to narrate what's nearby as you walk the event
  • Pocket your phone — audio guides you the rest of the way
  • Works for any place, not just curated landmarks — booths, stages, and pop-ups included
