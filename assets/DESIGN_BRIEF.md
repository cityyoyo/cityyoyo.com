# CityYoYo — Icon & Feature Graphic Design Brief

---

## App Icon

### Specs
- Master size: 1024 × 1024 px PNG (no alpha, no rounded corners — stores apply them)
- Export: 512 × 512 for Google Play

### Concept
The icon should communicate two things at a glance: **place** and **audio/story**.

### Direction
- **Primary element:** A map pin / location marker — the universal symbol for "place"
- **Secondary element:** A subtle audio or sound wave integrated into or around the pin — suggests listening, narration, story
- **Feel:** Clean, confident, modern. Not a photo, not a complex scene. Works at 60px.

### Color
- Background: Dark navy `#0f172a` — premium, map-like, distinctive on both light and dark home screens
- Pin / icon element: Warm amber/gold `#f59e0b` — warm, inviting, contrasts well on dark
- Optional accent: White for sound wave or highlights

### What to avoid
- Gradients that get muddy at small sizes
- Text in the icon
- Too many elements — one clear focal point only
- Generic map screenshot or photo background

### Inspiration keywords
- "audio pin", "story marker", "listen here"

## Android Feature Graphic

### Specs
- Size: 1024 × 500 px PNG or JPEG
- Shown at the top of the Play Store listing (when no promo video is set)

### Concept
A wide cinematic banner that works as a billboard — readable in 2 seconds.

### Layout
- **Left half:** Tagline text
  - Large: **"Every place has a story"**
  - Small below: "CityYoYo — Audio City Guide"
- **Right half:** A phone mockup showing the map screen (Shot 1), slightly angled

### Color
- Background: Dark navy `#0f172a` — consistent with icon
- Text: White headline, `#94a3b8` subline
- Accent: Amber `#f59e0b` on a word or underline (optional)

### What to avoid
- Cramming in feature bullet points — keep it to the tagline only
- Small text that can't be read at a glance
- Busy background (no map texture behind text — keep the left half clean)
