Explore Any Event in China, Hands-Free

Walking a festival, street market, or event district? Put on your headphones and let ChinaWalk narrate what's around you as you go — no VPN, no schedule, no searching.

How it works

  • Open Tour Mode and press your headphone button to start
  • ChinaWalk uses your location to narrate what's nearby as you walk the event
  • Place names are translated into your language
  • Works for any place, not just curated landmarks — booths, stages, and pop-ups included
