Two ways to explore

Tap a place, or just walk

📍

Explore Mode

Tap any spot on the map — a landmark, a café, a hidden alley — and instantly hear AI-generated narration crafted just for that place.

  • 1  Open the map and browse nearby places or search any city
  • 2  Tap any landmark, neighborhood, café, or point of interest
  • 3  Hear an accurate, engaging narration instantly
🎧

Tour Mode

Put your headphones on, pocket your phone, and walk. Tour Mode automatically finds and narrates interesting places as you pass them — no tapping, no screen time.

  • AirPods & headphone gestures — tap once for the next place, twice to pause, three times to skip
  • Personalized by your interests — cafés, history, art, architecture, and more
  • Background playback with lock-screen controls
  • Live captions follow along with every narration

FAQ

Common questions

How does CityYoYo generate its narrations?

CityYoYo uses AI to generate narration on the spot for whichever place you tap or walk past — nothing is pre-recorded or scripted in advance. Explore Mode narrates any point you tap on the map; Tour Mode automatically narrates nearby places hands-free as you walk.

Does CityYoYo work offline?

Yes. You can download city packs before you travel and use CityYoYo without an internet connection — useful for international travel or areas with spotty connectivity.

What is Tour Mode?

Tour Mode is a hands-free walking mode: put on your headphones, start walking, and CityYoYo automatically finds and narrates nearby places without you tapping the screen. It supports AirPods and headphone gestures (tap once for the next place, twice to pause, three times to skip), personalized interests, live captions, and background playback with lock-screen controls.

What languages does CityYoYo support?

CityYoYo's interface and narrations are available in English, Chinese, Korean, Spanish, Portuguese, and French.

Is CityYoYo free?

Yes, CityYoYo is free to download and use on iOS and Android, with optional higher-quality on-device AI voices.

Which cities does CityYoYo cover?

CityYoYo works in any city worldwide, not just major landmarks — including popular destinations across Japan, Korea, Vietnam, China, and beyond, plus cities in Europe and the Americas.
