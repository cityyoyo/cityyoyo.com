## Two Ways to Explore

### Explore Mode — Tap Any Place, Hear Its Story
Tap any spot on the map — a landmark, a café, a hidden alley — and instantly hear AI-generated narration crafted just for that place.

1. **Open the Map** — See what's around you. Browse nearby places or search any city in the world.
2. **Tap a Place** — Select any landmark, neighborhood, café, or point of interest.
3. **Listen** — Hear an accurate, engaging narration instantly.

### Tour Mode — Hands-Free Walking Tour
Put your headphones on, pocket your phone, and walk. Tour Mode automatically finds and narrates interesting places as you pass them — no tapping, no screen time required.

- AirPods & headphone gestures — tap once for the next place, twice to pause, three times to skip
- Personalized by your interests — cafés, history, art, architecture, and more
- Background playback with lock-screen controls
- Live captions follow along with every narration

## Feature Highlights

### Stories for Every Place
Fresh narrations generated on the spot — accurate, engaging, and unique to the place you're exploring. Nothing pre-recorded, nothing canned.

### Hands-Free Tour Mode
Walk and listen — CityYoYo finds and narrates nearby places automatically, with AirPods gestures and lock-screen controls.

### Offline City Packs
Download cities before you travel. Explore without internet — perfect for abroad or areas with limited connectivity.

### Multiple Voice Options
Choose from system voices or high-quality on-device voices. Your ears, your choice.

### 6 Languages
Available in English, 中文, 한국어, Español, Português, and Français. Switch anytime.

### Reading Mode
Prefer text? Flip to Reading Mode and browse narrations without audio.

### Simple by Design
No cluttered menus. No forced sign-ups. Open, pick a mode, explore.
