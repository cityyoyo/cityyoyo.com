FAQ

Common questions

Do I need a VPN to use a China travel app?

No. ChinaWalk is built to work in China without a VPN. It's been tested directly on the ground, on local carriers and wifi, without one. If you run into a network issue while traveling, the team wants to know — but a VPN is not a requirement to use the app.

How do I know what I'm looking at in China if I can't read Chinese?

ChinaWalk auto-translates on the spot. Tap a place and it generates a narration, then translates and speaks it in whichever language you've set for narration — English, Korean, Spanish, Portuguese, or French — so you don't need to read Chinese or run a separate translation app to understand what you're standing in front of.

Does a China travel app only cover famous landmarks?

Not with ChinaWalk. Nothing is pre-curated or pre-selected. Tap any place on the map — a hutong, a neighborhood teahouse, a random alley — and it generates a narration for that specific spot, the same as it would for a well-known landmark.

Can I even read the map when traveling in China?

Chinese maps commonly show place names only in Chinese characters, which is a real barrier before you even get to narration. ChinaWalk translates place names on the map and in place lists too, not just the narration content, so the label problem and the depth problem are both handled.

What is the best China travel app?

It depends what you need. For mobile payments, Alipay or WeChat Pay. For navigation, Amap (Gaode Maps), since Google Maps has thin coverage in China. For booking transport and hotels, Trip.com or DiDi. For on-the-spot text or camera translation, Pleco or Youdao. For understanding what you're looking at when you stop somewhere — landmark or not — ChinaWalk.
