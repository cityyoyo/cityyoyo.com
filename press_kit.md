# CityYoYo — Press Kit

## About CityYoYo

CityYoYo is an audio city guide for iOS and Android with two ways to explore. In Explore Mode, users tap any place on an interactive map and instantly hear a narration about that location — no pre-recorded content, no guided routes. In Tour Mode, users put on headphones and walk, and the app automatically finds and narrates nearby places hands-free, controllable with AirPods/headphone gestures. Offline packs mean no internet is required either way.

The app supports 6 languages (English, Simplified Chinese, Korean, Spanish, Portuguese, French) and offers multiple voice engines including on-device voices for high-quality playback without a network connection.

## Core Features

- **Fresh Narrations** — On-the-spot place descriptions, unique to every location
- **Explore Mode** — Interactive Mapbox map with tap-to-listen interaction
- **Tour Mode** — Hands-free walking tours; auto-narrates nearby places, controllable via AirPods/headphone gestures (next, pause, skip), personalized by interests, with background playback, lock-screen controls, and live captions
- **Offline Packs** — Downloadable city bundles for offline exploration
- **Voice Engines** — System and on-device voices
- **Multilingual** — 6 language UI + narration support
- **Reading Mode** — Text-only alternative to audio playback
- **Quiet Mode** — Browse without auto-play

## Target Audience

- Travelers exploring unfamiliar cities
- Curious locals who want to learn more about their neighborhood
- Language learners using audio narration in their target language
- Accessibility-focused users preferring audio over reading

## Technology

- AI narration via OpenAI and DeepSeek APIs with streaming
- On-device voice synthesis via ONNX-based Supertonic model
- Maps powered by Mapbox
- Offline content via bundled city packs
- Authentication via Firebase
